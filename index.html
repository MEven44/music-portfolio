Frequently Asked Questions

What style of music can we expect at our event?

I curate a balance of sophisticated Jazz standards, Latin bossa nova, timeless pop, and Broadway arrangements. The set is carefully structured to enhance conversation and atmosphere without ever overpowering your guests.

How long do you typically perform?

Most engagements range from 1 to 3 hours, covering cocktail hours, formal dinners, or guest arrivals. I can also compose a custom tailored song with personalized lyrics for your company or guest of honor upon request.

Do you bring your own sound equipment?

Yes. I perform on a premium professional digital piano and provide a high-end personal PA system. I arrive 90 minutes early to conduct sound checks. (If your venue has an acoustic grand piano, I am delighted to perform on it as well).

What is required to secure our event date?

A signed agreement and a booking retainer reserve your date exclusively. The remaining balance is due one week prior to the event, ensuring full focus on delivering an exceptional performance on your big day.
