Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of music do you offer?

I curate a blend of Jazz and contemporary instrumental pieces that enhance the atmosphere without distracting from conversation or programming. The music supports your event's tone—whether refined, inspiring, or celebratory.

How long do you typically play at events?

Most events include 1–3 hours of live piano music during cocktail hours, dinner service, or guest arrivals. We can design a custom schedule, and I can even craft a custom song with new words for your event.

Will amplification be needed when you perform?

I perform on an electric piano and bring my complete PA system, handling all setup and balance 90 minutes before the event start. (Unless the venue provides a tuned acoustic piano).

What is required to secure our event date?

A signed contract and booking fee reserve your date exclusively. The remaining balance is due one week before the event, allowing full focus on making your performance special.
