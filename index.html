Frequently Asked Questions

Does the venue need a piano?

No. If an acoustic piano is not available on-site, Moran brings a professional-grade digital keyboard and a clean, self-contained PA system suitable for any indoor or outdoor venue.

Can Moran learn specific songs for our event?

Yes. Custom requests are welcomed with advance notice. Moran can also write custom song lyrics dedicated to event hosts or honored guests.

What are the technical requirements?

Just one standard power outlet near the performance area. Moran handles all sound checks, cabling, and acoustic adjustments prior to guest arrival.

How far in advance should we book?

Due to event season demand across Northern California, dates are reserved on a first-come basis. Inquiring 2–6 months ahead is recommended.
