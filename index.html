MORAN EVEN

Tasteful Piano Music Tailored to Your Vision

"Every event has its own dynamic. My focus is listening to what you want, and providing music that lets your guests connect."

I have been playing jazz piano since I was 8 years old. Over my years as a performing musician, my work has spanned a wide range of settings—from composing music for the play Sappho, serving as Music Director for Cinderella, and performing as pianist for The Wizard of Oz and Gold Can't Love You Back at the House of Fates in Nevada City.

Based in the South Bay and performing throughout the Bay Area, I specialize in providing elegant, live piano for corporate gatherings, cocktail receptions, and private events. Whether utilizing your venue's grand piano or bringing my own self-contained audio setup, I ensure the music remains tasteful, beautiful, and never overly loud.
