About

For over six years, Moran Even has brought dynamic piano performances to life across Northern California—from intimate cocktail hours and high-profile corporate galas to theatrical pit orchestras for productions like Cinderella and The Wizard of Oz. Known for his rich jazz arrangements, Latin bossa nova grooves, and iconic pop hits, Moran seamlessly adapts to any event's atmosphere. Whether leading energetic open mics or setting an elegant backdrop for art openings, his playing delivers an unforgettable musical experience tailored to your guests.
